About the LAB

02
Architecture is not only a building.
It is a system for living, making and becoming.

S2DO Architectural LAB approaches architecture as a space between design practice and research. We investigate how buildings can respond to culture, climate, material innovation, emerging technology and changing ways of life.

From intimate residences to speculative habitats, each project begins with context and ends with a precise spatial idea.

01 / ObserveContext before form. Human experience before image.02 / ExperimentTest new materials, systems and spatial possibilities.03 / BuildTurn ideas into clear, useful and lasting architecture.
